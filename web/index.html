Tones

        
Tones 1 2 3 4 5 7 8 9. Tone 6 does not exist in standard Tâi-gí. Tones 4 and 8 are checked (束聲) — the syllable must end in p, t, k or h.

        
Typing ou in POJ input is read as oo (o͘) automatically.
